### 💡 The Vision Behind AuraPulse AI

Events are living, breathing, high-stakes operational systems. We built AuraPulse AI to bridge the gap between creative visual inspiration and real-world execution resilience:

1. **Aura Vision-to-BOQ Engine (Google Gemini 2.5 Flash)**:
   * Drop in any raw mood board or aesthetic inspiration image, or provide regional cultural parameters.
   * Gemini 2.5 Flash extracts structural trusses, lighting densities, seating ratios, and floral clouds via strict Pydantic schemas.
   * Instantly maps them into a standardized, 3-tier Bill of Quantities (BOQ):
     - 🔒 **Tier-1 (Critical)**: Guest safety, structural rigging, baseline catering, power.
     - ⚙️ **Tier-2 (Operational)**: Audio arrays, ambient lighting, guest comfort.
     - ✨ **Tier-3 (Discretionary)**: Exotic florals, cold spark fountains, photo booths.

2. **Dynamic Backwards Planning Timeline**:
   * Dynamically calculates target milestones counting backwards from the event date (`T-90d`, `T-60d`, ..., `T-0d`).
   * Near-term short-horizon tasks automatically compress into high-priority "Do Immediately" actions—no invalid past dates.

3. **Function-Calling Conversational Co-Pilot**:
   * Conversational replanner powered by Gemini tool calling: `scale_budget`, `add_item`, `remove_item`, and `set_event_date`.
   * Multi-turn chat with Hindi/Marathi language sensitivity and instant visual diff generation (+Added, ~Modified, -Removed).

4. **Reactive Contingency Solver**:
   * Real-time crisis simulation with one click:
     - 🌧️ *Flash Rainstorm Alert*
     - 💸 *Cut 20% Budget*
     - ⏰ *Baraat Delayed 1 Hour*
     - 🚨 *Caterer Abruptly Cancelled*
   * Locks all Tier-1 infrastructure, surgically trims Tier-3 luxury items to fund emergency assets (like all-weather German Pagoda marquees), and rebalances budgets deterministically.

5. **Google Services Tool Fabric**:
   * 🗺️ **Google Maps Places API (New) Grounding**: Discovers verified local backup vendors with authentic Google review ratings, addresses, and phone contacts.
   * 📅 **RFC-5545 Calendar & Web Intents**: Instant `.ics` file download and zero-permission Google Calendar web intent synchronization.
