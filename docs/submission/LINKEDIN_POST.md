### 💡 The Vision Behind AuraPulse AI

Events are living, breathing, high-stakes operational systems. We built AuraPulse AI to bridge the gap between creative visual inspiration and real-world execution resilience:

1. **Aura Vision-to-BOQ Engine (Gemini 2.5 Flash)**:
   * Drop in any raw mood board or aesthetic inspiration image.
   * Gemini 2.5 Flash deconstructs visual pixels into structural trusses, lighting densities, seating ratios, and floral clouds.
   * Instantly maps them into a standardized, 3-tier Bill of Quantities (BOQ):
     - 🔒 **Tier-1 (Critical)**: Safety, power, structural rigging, baseline catering.
     - ⚙️ **Tier-2 (Operational)**: Audio line arrays, ambient wash lights, guest comfort.
     - ✨ **Tier-3 (Discretionary)**: Exotic imported blooms, cold sparks, photo booths.

2. **Pulse Dynamic Engine (Gemini 2.5 Pro Reasoner)**:
   * Real-time contingency simulation with one click:
     - 🌧️ *Flash Rainstorm Alert*
     - 💸 *Cut 20% Budget*
     - ⏰ *Baraat Delayed 1 Hour*
     - 🚨 *Caterer Abruptly Cancelled*
   * Locks all Tier-1 infrastructure, surgically trims Tier-3 luxury items to fund emergency assets (like all-weather German Pagoda marquees), and cascades timelines without breaking kitchen curfews.

3. **Google Services Tool Fabric**:
   * 🗺️ **Google Maps Places API Grounding**: Discovers verified local backup vendors with live Google review ratings and phone contacts.
   * 📊 **Google Sheets API v4**: Generates dynamic formula-driven spreadsheets with live `=SUM()` and category `=SUMIF()` calculations.
   * 📅 **Google Calendar API v3**: Syncs chronological run-of-shows with weather-vulnerability alarm tags.
